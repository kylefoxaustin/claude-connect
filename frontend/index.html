    🛑 Wind down the fleet

    The mirror of session start: startup orients a session; wind-down persists it. Each session posts its open findings, writes its memory, commits dirty repos, and releases its leases — then posts a verified ack (the bus checks git + leases on disk; it does not take the session’s word for it). A session that is asking you a question or mid-task is never interrupted or closed — it is waited for. You close only what has provably wound down.

      ⟳ Relaunch dormant sessions

      Each resumes with claude --continue in its own folder — same conversation, no “new vs restart” prompt. Launches are staggered (one at a time) so 20 Claudes don’t stampede the box. Heavy sessions may auto-compact on resume — sort by tokens to see which those are.

        🧯 Reconstitute the fleet

        Reads the disaster-recovery roster and, per session, what it takes to bring it back: present (relaunch) · clone (git clone, then relaunch) · transcript-only (no repo — resume the conversation in a fresh dir). Already-live sessions are shown but not selectable. Extract the fleet-transcripts asset first (RESTORE.md) or a session resumes blank — missing transcripts are flagged.
